You don't need GTA 6 installed on the console in front of you to play it. Rockstar says the game will be playable through Xbox Cloud Gaming, including on Xbox One, and supports Remote Play on both PlayStation and Xbox.

## What it is

There are two ways to play GTA 6 away from a local install:

- **Xbox Cloud Gaming** streams the game from Microsoft's servers. Rockstar says it works on Xbox Series X|S and on **Xbox One**, even though GTA 6 isn't released natively for Xbox One.
- **Remote Play** streams the game from your own console to another device over your network or the internet. Rockstar says GTA 6 fully supports it on PlayStation and Xbox.

## How it works

- **Cloud uses Series S settings.** When you play through Xbox Cloud Gaming, the game runs with Xbox Series S graphics settings, whatever device you're streaming to.
- **Your connection matters.** Rockstar warns that connection speed and other technical factors can affect cloud performance in ways that wouldn't happen when playing locally.
- **Remote Play needs your own console.** Your PS5 or Xbox Series X|S runs the game and streams it to you, so the console has to be available and online. Check PlayStation's or Xbox's Remote Play setup guide for your device.

## Tips

- **Use a wired connection where you can.** For cloud and Remote Play alike, a cable or a strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi signal usually means less lag.
- **Still on Xbox One?** Cloud gaming is your route into GTA 6, but test your connection first. Rockstar itself says results can vary.
- **Remote Play is a good backup** for launch night if someone else needs the TV.

## What's not confirmed yet

- Which subscription you need for Xbox Cloud Gaming access to GTA 6. Check Microsoft's current requirements.
- Whether saves carry across between local play and cloud play. Rockstar hasn't covered this for GTA 6.
- Phone, tablet or browser support details for streaming.

## FAQ

### Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Only through Xbox Cloud Gaming. Rockstar says the game is available via cloud on Xbox One consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings.

### Does GTA 6 support Remote Play?

Yes. Rockstar says GTA 6 fully supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox.

### Will cloud gaming look as good as playing locally?

It uses Xbox Series S graphics settings, and Rockstar notes that connection speed can affect performance.

### Is GTA 6 on PS4?

No. GTA 6 is released for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One is supported only through cloud gaming.
