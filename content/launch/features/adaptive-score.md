GTA 6's original score is by Justice, the French electronic duo. It's adaptive: the music changes depending on how you play, rather than looping one track per area.

## What it is

The score is the music that isn't coming from a radio: the background tracks that build tension in a chase or ease off when things are calm. GTA 6's score is composed by Justice (Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay). They're a Grammy-winning duo whose song "Flower in a Hurricane" is in the Launch Trailer.

## How it works

Rockstar's announcement of the score describes compositions that are dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with Vice City and Leonida throughout Jason and Lucia's story.

In practice, that means the music is meant to react to what you're doing. The official text doesn't say exactly what triggers a change (combat, chases, missions or free roam), so treat any specific breakdown you read elsewhere as unconfirmed until people have played the game.

Outside the game, there's a separate release. **Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score** arrives on 11 December 2026 on streaming services, CD and vinyl. It has 30 tracks, including "Flower in a Hurricane" (featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard) and "Pusher" (featuring Gesaffelstein).

The score isn't the same as **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album**, the soundtrack album of original songs by other artists.

## Tips

- **Play once with the music up.** If you usually turn the score down for the radio, give it a session first. It's designed to follow the action.
- **Check the audio settings.** Rockstar games usually let you set music, dialogue and effects volumes separately. GTA 6's exact options aren't confirmed yet.
- **Want to hear it outside the game?** The score album comes out on 11 December, three weeks after launch.

## What's not confirmed yet

- What exactly makes the score change (wanted level, combat, mission stages and so on).
- Whether you can turn the score off on its own, separately from the radio.
- Which tracks from the album play where in the game.
