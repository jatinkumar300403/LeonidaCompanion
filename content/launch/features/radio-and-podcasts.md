In GTA 6, radio is no longer something you only hear in a car. You can listen on foot through headphones or earbuds connected to Jason's or Lucia's phone. The game also adds on-demand podcasts, a first for the series.

## What it is

Radio has always been the main way music reaches you in GTA. According to Rockstar's Newswire post on GTA 6's in-game radio, Leonida has the series' biggest and widest-ranging radio selection yet, with stations covering everything from classic rock to talk radio.

On top of normal broadcast radio, GTA 6 adds podcasts. They're on demand, so you choose what plays instead of tuning in to whatever is on air.

## How it works

Here's what Rockstar has confirmed so far:

- **Radio and podcasts work in vehicles and on foot.** Out of a car, you listen through headphones or earbuds connected to your character's smartphone.
- **Podcasts are separate from radio.** Rockstar describes them as something Jason and Lucia can listen to beyond Leonida's terrestrial radio.
- **Six stations have been named so far:** Cocoteo FM (modern Latin music), Back Country Radio (country), AfroBank FM (African music, classic and contemporary), The Chamber 106.6 (metal), Flash FM (pop) and Dirty South Classics (Southern rap and hip-hop).
- Each named station has its own hosts, and you can hear previews in the Music section of Rockstar's official GTA 6 site.

## Tips

- **Listen to the previews first.** If you already know which stations you like, you'll find them faster in-game.
- **Headphones help in real life too.** If you play with headphones on PlayStation 5, Rockstar says the game supports Tempest 3D Audio for in-game sound.
- **Try a podcast on a long drive or a walk.** On-demand audio suits long stretches of exploring better than live radio.

## What's not confirmed yet

- The full list of stations and how many there are.
- Which podcasts exist and how many episodes each one has.
- Exactly how you put headphones on, or switch stations on foot, from the phone.
- Whether you can build a custom playlist.

We'll update this page once the game is out and these details are known.

## FAQ

### Can you listen to the radio on foot in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar says radio and podcasts work on foot through headphones or earbuds connected to your character's smartphone.

### What are podcasts in GTA 6?

On-demand shows Jason and Lucia can listen to beyond regular radio. Rockstar calls it a first for the series. It hasn't listed the shows yet.

### How many radio stations does GTA 6 have?

Not confirmed yet. Rockstar has named six so far and calls it the biggest radio selection in the series.

### Where can I hear the stations before launch?

In the Music section of Rockstar's official Grand Theft Auto VI site, which has previews of the six named stations.
