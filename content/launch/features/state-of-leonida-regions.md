GTA 6 takes place in Leonida, a fictional state that includes a modern-day Vice City. Rockstar's official site has shown six areas so far: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

## What it is

Leonida is GTA 6's map: a whole state rather than one city. Rockstar describes it as "the sprawling state of Leonida", marking the series' return to modern-day Vice City. The official site calls Vice City the darkest side of the sunniest place in America.

## How it works

The "Only in Leonida" section of Rockstar's official site has a tour of these areas:

- **Vice City.** The neon-lit city at the heart of the state.
- **Leonida Keys.** Also named in official screenshots.
- **Grassrivers.** Also named in official screenshots. One of the radio stations, Back Country Radio, broadcasts to the Grassrivers and Port Gellhorn.
- **Port Gellhorn.**
- **Ambrosia.**
- **Mount Kalaga.**

Rockstar hasn't published a full map yet. On launch day (19 November 2026), it's adding a free printable map of Leonida to the official site, alongside a cover-art poster.

## Tips

- **Grab the free map on launch day.** It's a digital download from Rockstar's official GTA 6 site.
- **Take your time with the official tour.** The "Only in Leonida" pages are a spoiler-light way to get familiar with the area names before you play.
- **Keep a note of the places you want to visit.** It's easy to lose track of names on launch night.
