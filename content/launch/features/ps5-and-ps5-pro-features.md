On PlayStation 5, GTA 6 adds DualSense haptic feedback that you can adjust in the accessibility settings, Tempest 3D Audio and faster loading. PS5 Pro owners also get sharper image quality through PSSR, plus ray-traced shadows that only the Pro version has. On every console, the game targets 30 frames per second.

## What it is

Rockstar's 10 November 2026 Newswire post laid out how GTA 6 runs on each console. The game was built for current-generation hardware only: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. On top of that shared base, the PlayStation versions get some extras.

## How it works

**On every console**

- GTA 6 targets **30 frames per second**. Rockstar says it chose this to keep Leonida's world dense and detailed.

**PlayStation 5**

- **Haptic feedback** on the DualSense controller is built in across the whole game, and you can **adjust it in the accessibility settings**.
- **Spatial audio:** in-game sound is rendered for Tempest 3D Audio.
- **Improved load times**, according to Rockstar.

**PlayStation 5 Pro**

- **PSSR** (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) for a clearer image.
- **Ray-traced shadows**, which Rockstar says are only available on PS5 Pro.

## Tips

- **Turn haptics down if they bother you.** They're adjustable in accessibility settings, which helps if you're sensitive to vibration or play long sessions.
- **Use headphones for 3D audio.** Tempest 3D Audio is designed with headphones in mind.
- **Don't expect a 60 fps mode.** Rockstar's stated target is 30 fps. Whether there's a separate performance mode hasn't been announced.

## What's not confirmed yet

- Whether any console gets a performance or quality mode toggle.
- Resolution targets for each console.
- What the haptic settings look like (an on/off switch, a strength slider, or both).
- A PC version. Rockstar has only announced PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## FAQ

### Does GTA 6 run at 60 fps?

Rockstar says the game targets 30 frames per second on current consoles. A 60 fps mode hasn't been announced.

### What does PS5 Pro add to GTA 6?

Sharper image quality through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), and ray-traced shadows that are only on PS5 Pro.

### Can I turn off controller vibration in GTA 6?

Rockstar says DualSense haptic feedback can be adjusted through the accessibility settings. The exact options will be confirmed at launch.

### Does GTA 6 support 3D audio on PS5?

Yes. Rockstar says all in-game sounds take full advantage of Tempest 3D Audio.
