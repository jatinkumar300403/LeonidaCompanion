GTA 6's Ultimate Edition doesn't hand you everything at once. Rockstar says its bonus items are spread through Jason and Lucia's story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter. You can buy the Ultimate Edition outright, or add it later as an upgrade to the Standard Edition.

## What it is

GTA 6 comes in two editions: the Standard Edition and the Ultimate Edition. The Ultimate Edition is the game plus the **Ultimate Edition Upgrade**, a collection of extra vehicles, weapons, clothing, businesses and properties.

Rockstar Support lists the upgrade's contents as:

- '95 Grotti Cheetah
- Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers
- Personalized weapon variants
- Vice City styles
- Jason's safehouse vehicles
- Ganado retro build
- Rideout Customs mod shop
- Sara's Unisex Salon
- Shitzu Squalo
- Stock 305 clothing store
- '67 Vapid Dominator buggy and garage
- Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor
- One-Eyed Willie's mod shop
- Goodtime Gear
- PTT Youngin$ compound
- Classic car collection

## How it works

- **Items unlock as you progress.** Rockstar Support says the bonuses are threaded across the story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter. Expect them to appear as you play, not all on day one.
- **The Ultimate Edition is digital only.** There's no physical Ultimate Edition.
- **You can upgrade later.** Standard Edition owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately at any time from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.
- **Code-in-box copies can upgrade too**, once the download code has been redeemed.
- **Pre-order bonuses are separate.** The Vintage Vice City Pack comes with every pre-order or purchase made before 20 November 2026, whichever edition you buy.

## Tips

- **Not sure? Start with Standard.** The upgrade can be bought at any point, so you don't have to decide before launch.
- **Bought the code-in-box version?** Redeem the code first, then buy the upgrade on your platform's store.
- **Don't hunt for everything on day one.** If items show up behind story chapters, keep playing the story to see them.

## What's not confirmed yet

- Exactly which item unlocks at which chapter.
- How you claim each item in-game (for example, a message, a phone notification or a property simply becoming available).
- The Ultimate Edition's price. Rockstar's own sites don't list it, so check the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store in your region.

## FAQ

### Do you get every Ultimate Edition item straight away?

Probably not. Rockstar says the items are threaded across the story, with new ones uncovered behind each chapter.

### Can I upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later?

Yes. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately on the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store, and you can buy it at any time.

### Is there a physical Ultimate Edition?

No. The Ultimate Edition is digital only. Code-in-box owners can add the upgrade after redeeming their code.

### Is the Vintage Vice City Pack part of the Ultimate Edition?

No. It's a separate bonus for every pre-order or purchase made before 20 November 2026, on either edition.
