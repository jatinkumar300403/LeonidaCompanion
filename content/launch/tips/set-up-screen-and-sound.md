> [!NOTE]
> Pre-launch tip — will be updated after release.

Set up your TV and audio before you start GTA 6. It takes a few minutes and makes night-time Vice City much easier to see and hear.

1. **Run your console's HDR or display calibration** first, if your TV supports HDR.
2. **Then check the in-game brightness settings.** Rockstar's open-world games usually include a brightness or HDR calibration screen. Follow its on-screen guide.
3. **Turn on your TV's game mode** to cut input lag.
4. **Use headphones on PS5 if you can.** Rockstar says GTA 6's sound is rendered for Tempest 3D Audio.
5. **Balance the volume mix.** If dialogue gets lost under the music or radio, lower those first.

## Why it works

Calibrated brightness keeps dark scenes readable without washing out bright beaches, and clear audio helps you hear what's going on around you.
