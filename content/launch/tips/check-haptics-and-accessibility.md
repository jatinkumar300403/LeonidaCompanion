> [!NOTE]
> Pre-launch tip — will be updated after release.

Open GTA 6's accessibility settings before your first long session. Rockstar says DualSense haptic feedback on PS5 can be adjusted there.

1. **Open the pause menu** and look for the settings, then the accessibility section.
2. **Set haptic feedback** to a level that's comfortable for you. Turn it down if strong vibration tires your hands.
3. **Check the subtitles** while you're in the menus. Rockstar's recent open-world games have offered subtitle options; GTA 6's exact options will be confirmed at launch.
4. **Look at the controls page** too, so you know the layout before the first chase.
5. **Come back later.** Settings are easier to tune once you've played an hour.

## Why it works

Five minutes in the menus now saves you from changing settings mid-mission later. We'll list every accessibility option here once the game is out.
