> [!NOTE]
> Pre-launch tip — will be updated after release.

Turn on automatic downloads and leave your console in rest mode (PS5) or sleep mode (Xbox), so GTA 6 is installed and ready when it unlocks at midnight.

1. **Pre-order digitally, or redeem your code-in-box code.** Pre-loading opened on 10 November 2026: at midnight local time on PS5, and at 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox Series X|S.
2. **PS5:** turn on auto-downloads, then leave the console on or in Rest Mode.
3. **Xbox Series X|S:** turn on automatic updates, then leave the console on or in Sleep mode.
4. **Check that the download has finished** before launch night. The game unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November.
5. **Still stuck?** Rockstar's pre-load FAQ links to the PlayStation and Xbox help pages.

## Why it works

Rockstar Support says automatic pre-load only runs when these console settings are on and the console isn't fully switched off.
