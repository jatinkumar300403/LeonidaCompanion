> [!NOTE]
> Pre-launch tip — will be updated after release.

Clear at least 212 GB of free space before you pre-load. Rockstar says the GTA 6 pre-load needs up to that much, depending on your console.

1. **Check your free space** in your console's storage settings.
2. **Delete games you've finished or won't play this month.** Your saves usually stay in the cloud or on the console, but check before you delete anything you care about.
3. **Clear out old captures.** Video clips and screenshots add up fast.
4. **Leave some headroom.** Day-one patches and later updates may need extra space on top of the pre-load.
5. **Start early.** A download this big can take hours on a slow connection.

## Why it works

If there isn't enough space, the pre-load won't start or finish, and you could be stuck downloading after the game unlocks at midnight on 19 November.
