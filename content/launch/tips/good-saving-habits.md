Don't rely on autosave alone. Save manually whenever the game lets you, especially before a big job.

1. **Look for the save icon.** Most open-world games show one while autosaving. Don't quit or power off while it's on screen.
2. **Save before risky jobs.** If something goes wrong, you can go back to a clean point.
3. **Use more than one save slot** if the game offers them, so one bad save can't cost you hours.
4. **Quit from the menu,** not by closing the game, so your progress is written properly.
5. **Keep cloud saves on** in your console settings as a backup.

## Why it works

Autosaves usually happen at checkpoints, not whenever you'd like them. A manual save, and a second slot, protect your progress from mistakes and crashes.
