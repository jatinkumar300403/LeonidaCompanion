Every pre-order or purchase of GTA 6 made before 20 November 2026 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack, on both editions and on digital or code-in-box copies.

According to Rockstar Support, the pack contains a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan with a garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. Digital pre-orders also come with one month of GTA+.
