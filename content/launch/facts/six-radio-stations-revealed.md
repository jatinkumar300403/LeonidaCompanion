Rockstar has named six of GTA 6's in-game radio stations so far: Cocoteo FM (modern Latin), Back Country Radio (country), AfroBank FM (African music), The Chamber 106.6 (metal), Flash FM (pop) and Dirty South Classics (Southern rap and hip-hop).

Rockstar calls it the series' biggest and widest-ranging radio selection yet, so expect more stations than these six.
