# Content guide — voice, formats, rules

## Voice
- Direct, friendly, zero fluff. Answer first, explain second.
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Phone-readable.
- No hype words ("insane", "ultimate") in body text. Titles can have energy, bodies stay useful.
- Never pretend to be official. Never say "Rockstar told us".

## Formats

### Tip (target: 80–250 words)
- **Title formula**: verb + outcome + context → "How to Lose a 3-Star Wanted Level Fast in GTA 6"
- **Summary** (≤ 160 chars): the answer in one line.
- **Body**: 1-sentence answer → 3–6 numbered steps or bullets → "Why it works" (optional) → source line.
- Tags: 1–3 from the tag list. Spoiler level set honestly.

### Feature explainer (300–800 words)
- Title: "How [mechanic] Works in GTA 6" / "GTA 6 [mechanic] Explained"
- Sections: What it is → How it works → Tips → What's not confirmed yet → FAQ (3–5 Q/A, which also generates FAQ rich results).

### Fact (1–3 sentences + sources)
- One fact per article. A status badge is required:
  - **Confirmed**: an official Rockstar source link is required.
  - **Verified**: 2+ independent reliable sources, or seen directly in multiple gameplay streams (link the VODs with timestamps).
  - **Community**: from a solved forum thread (link it).
- Trivia and easter eggs are facts too. Tag them `easter-eggs`.

## Sourcing rules
- Every tip, feature and fact lists its sources. Streamer discoveries: credit the streamer by name and link the VOD/clip with a timestamp.
- Write in your own words. Never paste paragraphs from other sites. Wiki text is only used by Ask AI with attribution.
- Facts are never based on leaks, datamines of unreleased content, or rumours.

## Spoilers
- `none`: safe for anyone. `minor`: early-game content, mild surprises. `major`: story beats, characters' fates, endings.
- Major-spoiler content always sits behind `||spoiler||` blocks, and the title itself must never spoil anything.
- Rockstar's posting policy targets story spoilers, endings and isolated cutscenes. Don't build content around them.

## Clips curation
- Approve: funny chaos, physics fails, insane stunts, clutch escapes, wholesome moments, 15–60s.
- Reject: isolated cutscenes, story/ending reveals, cheating/mods/glitch exploits, leaks, NSFW, harassment of real people, clips from non-GTA VI streams.
- The creator is always credited (automatic). Removal requests are honoured within 24h, no argument.

## Online mode
- Until Rockstar announces it: only the "coming soon" page + waitlist. No speculation articles presented as facts.
- Never publish money glitches, exploits, mod menus or "how to get unbanned" content.

## Cadence (launch week)
- Launch night: 10–15 quick tips, 20+ approved clips, every forum question answered within 30 min.
- Days 2–7: 5–10 tips/day driven by `/admin/gaps`, 2 feature explainers/day, weekly top 10 clips on Monday.
- Update older tips when the community finds something better. Bump "updated" and keep the URL.
